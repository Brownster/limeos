## First independently workable milestone

Deliver a registered deterministic alert/schedule/notification vertical slice: bounded schedule creation/read/pause, maintenance windows, deterministic due calculation, one durable occurrence/job identity, transition-only alerts and an in-app notification outbox with visible success/failure/acknowledgment. Start with observation-only alerts and notification jobs; installed storage effects are unnecessary. Define backup/protection/container/APT operation adapters now, but mark unavailable adapters blocked until registered execution exists. A missing executor must produce an explicit blocked state.

Persist occurrence reservation and job creation atomically through the shared Store. Use a unique `(schedule id, revision, due occurrence)` identity, restart reconciliation and explicit missed-run/coalescing policy; handle concurrent ticks, clock rollback/forward and maintenance-window boundaries. Reads never write. Keep queue, retry, retention and notification payload bounds explicit. External delivery cannot promise exactly-once without a receiver's idempotency support; retain the occurrence/delivery key and show ambiguous delivery honestly.

Every scheduled effect resolves current grants and fresh evidence at dispatch and effect time. A one-time human approval token cannot authorize repeated runs. Recurring approval must be a separately reviewed, revocable policy covering the exact operation/resource set, maximum frequency, maintenance window, expiry and failure/stop limits. Destructive/elevated changes still require fresh approval unless a specifically authorized narrow runbook applies. Revision, resource or privilege changes suspend execution for review.
