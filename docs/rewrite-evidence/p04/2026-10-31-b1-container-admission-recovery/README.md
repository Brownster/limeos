## Observed behavior

Actual owned Unix listeners count zero protected-reader requests for not-due,
paused or failed host/manage admission and for expired/revoked queued jobs.
Successful fixtures use private unit credentials/clocks; production root socket,
peer, nonce and original time checks remain unchanged. Actual concrete member
grants are necessarily resolved after collection and reauthenticated as one
complete set inside completion. Missing grants refuse the sample.

The Store permit retains the original owner, policy/schedule/grant revision,
nonce, generation and deadline while binding the actual fresh full-ID members.
Original owner exclusion applies before root access. Source wall age and
legacy/retained subject checks are shared by poll and queued completion. A partial
container pass cannot silently retire a retained subject or claim complete health.

Genuine Store reopen proves the original queued job completes once with its
original identity, one partial result and one in-app publication. Repeat recovery
returns the original receipt without another RPC or durable job. Expired or
revoked jobs use independently rechecked discard before any read. Failed source
collection changes no job/result state and allows immediate fresh retry of the
same queued job; ordinary poll retry is also covered.

Actual database-worker blockage proves admission can expire without contacting
the reader, and completion can expire after a successful RPC without a late
sample. A real SQLite write-lock wait similarly expires the narrowed read
deadline without writes. A longer supplied deadline cannot extend original
admission. Old-generation or old-nonce releases cannot free newer work.
Cancellation retains only the original bounded lease until expiry; a fresh
read then succeeds. Failure cleanup has a separate 100-ms best-effort bound,
settles no job and starts no detached work.
