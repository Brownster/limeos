## Fresh device verification and installed readiness

Commit `15e4217` adds `executor-storage`, typed `storage-check`/`storage-wait` commands and a dormant package-owned readiness service. Fresh sysfs device numbers, partition/slave relationships and direct descriptor-based blkid probes replace cached dashboard/udev identity. The verifier excludes every connected backing device beneath root, boot/firmware/EFI and their aliases, requires unambiguous UUID and optional ancestry serial, and checks an exact writable filesystem mount with descriptor statx mount/device IDs. Symlinks, writable path ancestors, foreign filesystems and unsupported mappings fail closed. No storage mutation is registered. [Operations](../../p04-storage-readiness.md) and the [boundary review](storage-boundary-review.md) state the remaining limits.

The initial installed service exposed a real context defect: with no capabilities, UID 0 cannot read PID 1's namespace symlink. [Diagnosis](storage-context-diagnosis.json) reproduced that denial while proving the mount tables and raw descriptor probe remained readable.
